1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose
This document specifies the software requirements for Unikart version 2.0. It defines the system scope, actors, functional requirements, business rules, lifecycle behaviour, external interfaces, non-functional requirements, data model, critical use cases and acceptance criteria. It is intended for the project guide, development team, testers and evaluators. IEEE Std 830-1998 is used as a structural guide; it is not treated as a mandatory current standard.
1.2 Scope
Unikart is a business-to-consumer (B2C) platform for colleges and universities. Unikart operates its own physical store inside each participating institution and provides a web platform through which authorised institutional users can purchase, borrow and sell physical books, notes and study material.
Buy books, notes and study material from Unikart stores.
Borrow physical items for 14 days, 1 month or 45 days against a configurable non-refundable borrow fee and refundable deposit.
Sell books and study material to Unikart through an in-person store visit; approved payouts are credited to the user wallet.
Receive items through self pickup from the customer’s own institution store or through delivery, including cross-institution transactions.
Use the wallet for eligible payments, fines and dues, and redeem the balance to a validated bank account.
All stock is owned by Unikart. Customers cannot create listings or transact directly with one another; the platform is therefore not a peer-to-peer marketplace.
Out of scope for v2.0: peer-to-peer lending or selling, online submission of items for sale, digital e-book/PDF content, and operation of Unikart’s own card/UPI payment instrument.

2. Overall Description
2.1 Product Perspective
Unikart is a self-contained web system serving multiple participating institutions through one platform. Each institution has exactly one Unikart store in the v2.0 scope. Inventory belongs to stores, while catalogue search is platform-wide. A checkout can contain items from multiple stores and can mix purchase and borrow lines; the system splits such a checkout into independent StoreOrders so stock, fulfilment, delivery, cancellation and completion are store-scoped.

2.2 Product Functions
Institution-scoped registration, authentication and RBAC.
Cross-institution catalogue search with store-level availability.
Multi-store checkout supporting purchase and borrow lines in the same cart.
Borrow fee, deposit, automatic copy allocation, renewal, return, reminders and fines.
Self pickup and partner delivery/return pickup, including cross-institution delivery.
Sell-to-Unikart workflow with in-store verification, valuation, wallet payout and listing.
Wallet payments, mixed wallet + gateway payment, redemption and financial ledgering.
Cancellation/refund with store-order level settlement.
Copy-level inventory and Item-level store listings.
Notifications, administration, configurable rules, reporting, audit logging and operational monitoring.

Only authorised users from participating institutions may register and transact.
Only physical items/copies are supported in v2.0.
Selling to Unikart requires an in-person store visit.
Card/UPI/net-banking data is handled by the payment gateway; Unikart does not store card numbers.
Wallet, order, deposit and stock changes must be transactional and durable.
Business parameters must be configurable and versioned; existing transactions use the parameter snapshot effective at the time they were created.

4. Order and Borrow Lifecycle
4.1 Checkout and StoreOrder Model
A cart is converted into one Checkout parent. The system groups cart lines by owning store and creates one StoreOrder per store. Each StoreOrder independently tracks fulfilment, delivery, cancellation, refund and completion. This removes the earlier singular-store ambiguity while preserving a consolidated checkout view for the customer.

4.3 Purchase Lifecycle
Purchase lines follow Payment Pending → Confirmed → fulfilment (Ready for Pickup or Dispatched) → Handed Over → Completed. A purchase may be cancelled before handover. After handover the sale is final. In a mixed StoreOrder, purchase lines complete at handover while borrow lines enter Active and continue independently.

4.5 Invalid Transition Control
Every state-changing API/service operation shall validate the current state before applying a transition. An invalid transition shall return a deterministic business error, shall not mutate inventory or money, and shall create an audit/operational log entry. Repeated delivery, payment or wallet callbacks shall be treated as idempotent when they carry the same provider event or idempotency key.

6.5 Communication Interfaces
All client-server and server-to-service communication shall use HTTPS with TLS 1.2 or higher and JSON-based REST APIs. Provider callbacks shall be authenticated and signature-verified. Correlation IDs shall be included in application logs for payment, delivery, wallet and order operations.
6.6 Integration Failure Behaviour
Unknown provider status shall never be treated as success or failure without a verified callback or reconciliation result.
Payment timeout: keep transaction Pending and reconcile asynchronously; do not create a second charge using a different idempotency key for the same checkout attempt.
Delivery API failure: retry according to policy; after retry limit, move the job to operational exception and notify store staff.
Wallet payout failure: release reserved wallet amount and record Failed state; no silent balance loss.
Notification failure: retry; if a mandatory notice fails on one channel, use an available fallback channel.

UC-03 — Place a borrow order
Field
Specification
Primary Actor
Customer
Preconditions
Customer is authenticated; listing is borrowable; Customer is not blocked by an unresolved undisputed due; selected duration is valid.
Main Flow
1. Customer adds borrow line to cart. 2. System shows fee, deposit and eligible fulfilment options. 3. Customer confirms Checkout; system creates StoreOrder(s). 4. System validates availability and locks eligible Copy rows atomically. 5. Payment is authorised using wallet/gateway/both. 6. On successful payment the borrow line is confirmed and Copy is allocated. 7. StoreOrder moves to Ready for Pickup or Dispatched. 8. At handover the borrow start time and due date are created.
Alternate / Exception Flows
Copy unavailable: affected line is removed/refunded and customer may confirm revised total. Payment timeout: transaction remains Pending until verified. Concurrent conflict: only one confirmed allocation succeeds.
Postconditions
Confirmed StoreOrder, Payment/Allocation records, BorrowRecord and notifications exist.

UC-04 — Place a purchase order
Field
Specification
Primary Actor
Customer
Preconditions
Customer authenticated; listing is active; product is available at checkout.
Main Flow
1. Add purchase line. 2. Review store split, fulfilment and total. 3. Confirm payment. 4. System atomically allocates a Copy. 5. Store prepares pickup or creates delivery job. 6. Handover is verified. 7. Purchase line becomes Completed.
Alternate / Exception Flows
Unavailable line: no allocation; refund that line; other StoreOrders continue only after revised-total confirmation. Delivery failure: retry, then cancel/return if limit reached.
Postconditions
Handover recorded; Copy marked Sold; refund only if cancellation/failure before handover.

UC-08 — Return borrowed item and settle deposit/fine
Field
Specification
Primary Actor
Customer / Store Manager / Delivery Partner
Preconditions
Borrow is Active or Overdue; customer has a valid return route.
Main Flow
1. Customer chooses counter return or pickup. 2. System schedules pickup if required. 3. Copy is received and moves to Inspection. 4. Store Manager records condition. 5. System calculates late/damage/loss fine. 6. Deposit is reduced by fine/deductions. 7. Remaining refundable deposit is settled and borrow closes.
Alternate / Exception Flows
Fine exceeds deposit: shortfall becomes Due. Customer disputes fine: dispute record created; undisputed due remains enforceable.
Postconditions
Borrow closed, Copy status restored/withdrawn/lost as appropriate, refund ledger created, notifications sent.

UC-09 — Cancel StoreOrder
Field
Specification
Primary Actor
Customer
Preconditions
StoreOrder not handed over.
Main Flow
1. Customer selects a StoreOrder to cancel. 2. System validates state. 3. Copies are released. 4. Refund is calculated from PaymentAllocation and incurred delivery charges. 5. Gateway/wallet refunds are executed. 6. Customer is notified; audit entry is created.
Alternate / Exception Flows
Multi-store checkout: only selected StoreOrder is cancelled. Delivery already incurred: eligible refund reduced by actual charge.
Postconditions
StoreOrder Cancelled; inventory available; refund status recorded.

UC-10 — Sell items to Unikart
Field
Specification
Primary Actor
Customer / Store Manager
Preconditions
Customer authorised; in-person store visit.
Main Flow
1. Verify identity. 2. Inspect authenticity and condition. 3. Record valuation quote. 4. Customer accepts or declines. 5. If accepted, Manager approves. 6. Wallet is credited. 7. Approved item becomes a Copy and Listing.
Alternate / Exception Flows
Verification/rejection: no wallet credit and no listing. Seller declines: submission closed without purchase.
Postconditions
Submission retains audit trail; approved Copy appears in inventory.

UC-11 — Redeem wallet balance to bank
Field
Specification
Primary Actor
Customer
Preconditions
Wallet has sufficient available balance; bank account is validated.
Main Flow
1. Customer requests redemption and completes re-authentication/OTP. 2. System creates Pending redemption and reserves amount. 3. Payout request sent with idempotency key. 4. Success: settle wallet debit and mark redemption Success. 5. Failure: release reservation and mark Failed.
Alternate / Exception Flows
Unknown provider status: retain Pending and reconcile; do not double-submit. Previously successful result reversed: create reversal ledger entry.
Postconditions
Wallet balance reconciled; payout status and ledger linked.

UC-12 — Assess / dispute / resolve fine
Field
Specification
Primary Actor
Customer / Store Manager / Platform Admin
Preconditions
Fine exists and notification delivered.
Main Flow
1. Store Manager records fine. 2. Customer views breakdown. 3. Customer may dispute within 7 days. 4. Store Manager reviews and responds. 5. Platform Admin may finalise escalation. 6. Fine is upheld, adjusted or waived; ledger/dues updated.
Alternate / Exception Flows
Disputed amount does not add a new borrow block until resolution. Admin adjustment creates audit before/after values.
Postconditions
Final fine status and due balance are consistent across wallet/order/borrow.

UC-18 — Checkout multi-store / mixed cart
Field
Specification
Primary Actor
Customer
Preconditions
Cart contains one or more stores and may contain purchase + borrow lines.
Main Flow
1. System groups lines by store. 2. Creates one Checkout and one StoreOrder per store. 3. Calculates fulfilment per StoreOrder. 4. Customer reviews aggregated and per-store totals. 5. Payment allocations are recorded. 6. StoreOrders fulfil independently.
Alternate / Exception Flows
One StoreOrder cancelled: others continue. One unavailable line: revised total confirmation required before final capture where applicable.
Postconditions
Parent Checkout shows consolidated status; each StoreOrder is independently traceable.

UC-19 — Resolve concurrent checkout conflict
Field
Specification
Primary Actor
Customer / System
Preconditions
Two customers attempt to obtain same last eligible Copy.
Main Flow
1. Both requests reach checkout. 2. First transaction acquires allocation lock and confirms Copy after verified payment. 3. Second transaction observes no eligible Copy. 4. Second transaction receives deterministic conflict result and refund for affected amount.
Alternate / Exception Flows
Payment succeeds but stock conflict occurs: refund automatically; no duplicate allocation. Duplicate callback: idempotent response; no second allocation.
Postconditions
Exactly one active allocation exists for Copy; financial records balanced.

UC-20 — Handle delivery failure and retry/return
Field
Specification
Primary Actor
Customer / Store Manager / Delivery Partner
Preconditions
StoreOrder is Dispatched; delivery job active.
Main Flow
1. Delivery partner reports failure. 2. System records reason and increments attempt count. 3. If eligible, create next attempt. 4. After two retries/72 hours, return item to owning store. 5. Settle cancellation/refund using actual incurred charges. 6. Notify customer and store.
Alternate / Exception Flows
Provider API unavailable: retry API call without duplicate job creation. Customer refuses item: mark reason and follow same settlement policy.
Postconditions
DeliveryJob closed, Copy returned/released, StoreOrder settled and notifications sent.

Appendix A. Project Decisions and Assumptions
All previously unresolved high-impact ambiguities have been converted into explicit v2.0 decisions below. These are part of the final requirements baseline and should not remain open during implementation unless a formal change request is approved.
Decision ID
Topic
Final decision
D-01
Multi-store checkout
Allowed. One Checkout parent creates one StoreOrder per store. StoreOrders have independent fulfilment, cancellation, refund and status.
D-02
Mixed purchase + borrow cart
Allowed. A StoreOrder may mix purchase and borrow lines; one fulfilment method is selected per StoreOrder and all lines are handed over together.
D-03
Item–Copy–Listing model
Item is the product definition; Copy is the physical unit; Listing is a store-level Item offer and is not copy-specific.
D-04
Availability calculation
Derived by counting eligible Copies for the Item + Store + transaction type.
D-05
Concurrent checkout
Atomic allocation/locking guarantees at most one active allocation of a Copy.
D-06
Refund precedence
Gateway share returns to gateway; wallet share returns to wallet; mixed refund uses PaymentAllocation.
D-07
Borrow fee on no-show/cancellation
Borrow fee is non-refundable. Deposit is refundable subject to no-show deduction or delivery/cancellation charges.
D-08
Delivery retries
Two customer-unavailable/refusal retries within 72 hours, after which the item returns to store and the StoreOrder settles.
D-09
Wallet redemption failure
Pending redemption reserves funds; failure releases reservation; successful payout settles debit; corrections use reversal entries.
D-10
Fine dispute
Customer may dispute within 7 days; dispute states are explicit; undisputed dues remain enforceable.
D-11
Notification preferences
Users may opt out of non-security SMS. Mandatory notices remain required through at least one supported channel.
D-12
Retention
Profile 24 months after closure; financial/order/wallet/audit 7 years; application/security logs 90 days; backup retention 35 days unless hold applies.
D-13
Recovery
RPO <=15 minutes, RTO <=2 hours, quarterly restore test.
D-14
Configuration
Parameters are versioned and effective-dated; transactions snapshot the applicable values.
D-15
API versioning
Public/internal integration contracts use /api/v1 baseline. Breaking changes require a new version and parallel migration period.
